About the Game

Explore a universe that fits in your pocket! Navigate tiny galaxies, terraform miniature planets, and discover life forms smaller than atoms. A relaxing exploration game with procedural generation and a chill synthwave soundtrack. Built in 48 hours with Godot Engine.
