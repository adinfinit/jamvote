About This Event

Ludum Dare 57 is a 48-hour game jam where participants create games from scratch based on the theme "Tiny Worlds". All entries will be voted on by fellow jammers across five categories: Theme, Enjoyment, Aesthetics, Innovation, and Bonus. Judges contribute 30% of the final score.
