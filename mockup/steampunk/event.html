Event Information

Welcome to Ludum Dare 57! This is a 72-hour game jam where participants create a game from scratch based on the theme "Tiny Worlds." Solo and team entries are welcome.

After the jam ends, all jammers and judges will vote on each entry across five categories: Theme, Enjoyment, Aesthetics, Innovation, and Bonus.

Judge votes count for 30% of the final score. Results will be revealed after voting closes.
