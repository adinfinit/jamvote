Pocket Universe

Download Jam Page

Explore a universe that fits in your pocket! Shrink down to microscopic scale and discover entire civilizations living on grains of sand. Features procedurally generated micro-worlds, real-time ecosystem simulation, and a hauntingly beautiful ambient soundtrack. Built with Godot 4 in 48 hours.
