Game Entry

Pocket Universe

Download  ·  Jam Page  ·  Facebook

Pocket Universe is a meditative exploration game set inside a snow globe. Players guide a tiny astronaut through layered biomes, each no larger than a teacup, discovering miniature ecosystems that react to touch and sound. The game combines procedural terrain generation with hand-painted textures to create a world that feels both infinite and intimate.

Built in 48 hours using Godot Engine, the team focused on creating a sense of wonder through scale. The camera zooms seamlessly from a wide orbital view down to individual blades of grass, each blade hosting its own micro-civilization. A gentle ambient soundtrack adapts to the biome the player is currently exploring.

The controls are deliberately simple: move and interact. There are no enemies, no health bars, no fail states. The joy comes from discovery, from finding the tiny village hidden inside a mushroom cap, or the constellation of fireflies that spell out a secret message when viewed from above.
