About This Event

Welcome to the fifty-seventh edition of Ludum Dare, the world's longest-running online game jam. This weekend, developers from around the globe are building games inspired by the theme "Tiny Worlds." Whether you are crafting a microscopic adventure, a miniature city builder, or a pocket-sized platformer, we want to see your interpretation.

All entries will be judged by both fellow jammers and a panel of invited judges. The jammer and judge scores are blended at a 70/30 ratio. Each game is rated on five aspects: Theme, Enjoyment, Aesthetics, Innovation, and a Bonus category.

Remember: play and rate as many games as you can. The more votes each game receives, the more accurate and fair the final standings will be. Voting closes on Saturday, February 21 at midnight UTC.
