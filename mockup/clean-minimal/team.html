Pixel Pioneers

Pocket Universe

About the Game

Explore a universe that fits in your pocket. Shrink down to discover entire civilizations living on grains of sand. Navigate through microscopic landscapes, solve environmental puzzles, and help tiny creatures thrive in their minuscule habitats.

Built with Godot 4.3 in 72 hours. Features procedurally generated micro-worlds, a custom particle system, and an original chiptune soundtrack.
