About This Jam

Welcome to Ludum Dare 57! This is a 72-hour game jam where you create a game from scratch based on the theme. All skill levels are welcome.

Voting is open to all registered jammers. Play each game, rate it on five aspects, and leave constructive feedback. Judge scores account for 30% of the final score.
